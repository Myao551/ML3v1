# Interface Assets

## Courtyard

`courtyard.webp` is original artwork generated with the built-in OpenAI image
generation tool for this game's light theme. It is stored locally, 1536 x 1024,
and encoded as WebP at quality 85 (approximately 251 KiB).

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed background illustration for an actual playable Chinese four-player card game UI. Create a polished wide landscape 1536x1024 or 16:9, original cheerful Chinese casual mobile card-game art inspired by the welcoming bright atmosphere of Happy Landlord games. A sunny spring Chinese village courtyard, soft cyan blue sky, fluffy white clouds, jade green distant hills, small white houses with blue-gray tiled roofs at the left and right edges, a flowering peach tree entering upper right corner, vibrant green grass and a few pink flowers in lower corners. Center 55 percent remains a large open bright pale blue sky and very pale mint lawn for readable dark game UI overlaid later. Detailed charming hand-painted 2.5D illustration, premium mobile game background, round friendly architecture, afternoon sunlight, crisp shapes with painted shading, nuanced palette of sky blue, emerald, white, pink and small warm yellow accents. Calm central area, detailed outer edges. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no lettering, no playing cards, no characters, no UI or buttons, no bokeh or glowing orbs. Landscape fills entire image.

## Icons

The SVG files in `icons/` come from `lucide-static` version 0.468.0,
downloaded from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@0.468.0/icons/.
They are distributed under the license in `icons/LICENSE`.
No external icon or image requests are needed at runtime.
